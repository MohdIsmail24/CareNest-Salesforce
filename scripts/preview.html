Visiting Hours & Guidelines

Patient Visiting Guidelines

To ensure infection control and patient rest, visiting hours are strictly monitored:

  • General Wards: 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM Daily (Max 2 visitors)
  • Intensive Care Unit (ICU): 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM & 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM (1 visitor at a time)
  • Paediatric Ward: One parent permitted 24/7 alongside the child
  • Children under 12: Not permitted in ICU and post-operative wards
Digital visitor passes are issued at the Main Reception upon presenting valid government identification.
Admission Checklist

What to Bring for Admission

Please have the following ready during inpatient admission:

  • Government Photo ID: Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID
  • Insurance / TPA Card: For cashless hospitalization pre-authorization
  • Medical Records: Prior prescriptions, radiology scans, and lab reports
  • Regular Medications: In original packaging for clinical reconciliation
Our TPA & Cashless Insurance Desk is located on the Ground Floor, open 24/7.
